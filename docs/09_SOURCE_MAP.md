## Why `llms.txt` matters

A reviewer does not need to read dozens of repositories linearly. The AI-readable entry point allows third-party assistants to discover context packs, canonical sources, implementation material, and documentation. That is itself part of the propagation axis: reducing the human cost of explaining a very large body of work.
