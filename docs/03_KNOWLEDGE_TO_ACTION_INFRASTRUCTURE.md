# 03 — Knowledge-to-Action Infrastructure

Organizations usually already possess knowledge, expertise, procedures, discussions, and tools. The failure often occurs **between** them.

At every handoff, something can disappear: provenance, context, dissent, attribution, authority, rationale, responsibility, exceptions, or learning.

## Main systems

- **Koali / kOA-Linux-Koali** — host, trust, policy, lifecycle, local/offline operation, and deployment boundaries.
- **Konnaxion** — consultation, collaboration, structured deliberation, learning, coordination, and civic/public participation.
- **Smart Vote / EkoH** — information surfacing, contextual competence, and explicit alternative readings.
- **Orgo** — cases, tasks, workflows, roles, routing, escalation, closure, and operational memory.
- **Kristal** — structured, versioned, portable knowledge with provenance, attribution, relationships, and explicit state.
- **Interaction Kernel** — commands, queries, events, receipts, and reconciliation across systems that should remain independently authoritative.
- **UCKK** — learning, publication, media library, and reproduction of human capability.
- **Capsule Manager** — packaging, installation, lifecycle, diagnostics, and repeatable deployment.
- **Kintsugi** — integration of useful external systems without requiring kOA to rebuild everything or silently absorb their authority.
- **Worlds** — contextualized environments that make the architecture concrete for a particular institution, community, or scenario.
- **Ariane** — optional verified navigation for human or AI operators through existing interfaces.

## Simple division of labor

```text
Kristal = what the organization knows
Konnaxion = how people contribute, deliberate, and coordinate
Smart Vote / EkoH = what information rises to attention
Orgo = what the organization must now do
UCKK = how people learn to operate and reproduce the capability
Koali = how the environment is governed and operated
```

## Composition without absorption

The design goal is not "put everything in kOA." It is:

> **Allow heterogeneous systems to cooperate while preserving explicit ownership, authority, replaceability, and exit.**
