# 07 — Three Relevant Alumni Dialogues

This document does not assume that any of these people support kOA. It identifies why their publicly documented work intersects with different parts of the architecture.

## Stefania Kapronczay

Obama Foundation role: 2024–2025 Columbia University Scholar.

Her Foundation profile and recent Foundation conversation emphasize citizen participation, resilient democracy, consensus across differences, storytelling, local participatory programs, and community-organized local-history work.

**Natural kOA entry point: Axis 4 + Axis 3.**

```text
storytelling / participation → structured contribution → deliberation → community action → reusable memory
```

Relevant surfaces: Konnaxion, Worlds, UCKK, narrative/media layer, Kristal.

## Sándor Léderer

Obama Foundation role: 2018 Obama Fellow.

The Foundation describes K-Monitor's work around public spending and corruption data, civic-tech tools, public understanding and use of data, transparency, democracy, rule of law, and algorithmic risk indicators.

**Natural kOA entry point: Axis 2 + Axis 3.**

```text
public data → provenance → understandable signal → information surfacing → deliberation / scrutiny → accountable follow-through
```

Relevant surfaces: Smart Vote / EkoH, Kristal, Konnaxion, Orgo, audit and authority boundaries.

## Zuzanna Rudzinska-Bluszcz

Obama Foundation role: Leaders Europe 2020.

The Foundation describes her work around human and civic rights, strategic litigation, active citizenship, institutional protection of rights, and democratic participation.

**Natural kOA entry point: Axis 3 + governance safeguards.**

```text
claim / right / evidence → structured process → legitimate authority → decision → execution → recourse → institutional memory
```

Relevant surfaces: Kristal, Orgo, Konnaxion, explicit authority separation, receipts, reconstructibility, contestability, and recourse.
