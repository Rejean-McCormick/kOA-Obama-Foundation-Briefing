## What is different here

The Obama Foundation dossier is primarily **presentational and integrative**. Its first task is not to ask a question. Its first task is to show the machine clearly:

```text
linguistic infrastructure
+ information surfacing
+ knowledge-to-action infrastructure
+ diffusion / propagation
= a reusable architecture for collective organizational capacity
```
