# 02 — The Four Axes

## Axis 1 — Linguistic infrastructure

**Bottleneck:** language limits who can access, contribute to, validate, or reuse knowledge.

**Response:** structured semantics first; language realization second.

SemantiK Architect, Grammatical Framework, and structured concept identity are used to make the semantic object more stable than any one wording. GF provides a high-precision realization path where grammar and lexicon coverage are available. Coverage and quality still vary by language and domain.

The important architectural claim is:

> **One human language does not need to become the authoritative semantic source.**

A well-supported language is also a propagation corridor: it can open a new community, validators, corpora, and institutions.

## Axis 2 — Surfacing information

**Bottleneck:** once information is abundant, attention becomes scarce.

**Response:** Smart Vote, EkoH, provenance, and explicit lenses.

Smart Vote is treated as a general information-surfacing primitive, not only as an electoral mechanism. Objects can include ideas, solutions, resources, arguments, methods, plans, creative works, educational materials, destinations, candidates, or other option sets where a legitimate process permits.

The safeguards are as important as the ranking function:

```text
raw participation ≠ expert interpretation
popularity ≠ competence
competence in one domain ≠ universal expertise
advice ≠ final authority
```

## Axis 3 — Knowledge-to-action infrastructure

**Bottleneck:** organizations lose continuity between what they know, choose, do, and learn.

**Response:** specialized systems connected by explicit contracts and ownership boundaries.

```text
know → deliberate → decide → execute → remember → know better
```

## Axis 4 — Diffusion, transmission, and propagation

**Bottleneck:** complex systems fail to spread when every new person needs the founder to explain, demonstrate, install, and contextualize them.

**Response:** multiple cognitive and distribution corridors: technical documentation, education, fiction, books, music, video, Worlds, Cinematic Engine, public websites, GitHub, AI-readable context packs, and a constellation of public accounts.

## Combined effect

```text
Language makes meaning reachable.
Surfacing makes useful contributions discoverable.
Infrastructure makes collective action operable.
Propagation makes the capability transmissible.
```
